# Hoplane Code of Conduct

**Hoplane by [Kitgenix](https://kitgenix.com)**  
**Project repository:** https://github.com/Kitgenix/hoplane

## 1. Our commitment

Hoplane welcomes people who want to build, maintain, document, translate, test, and support the project. We are committed to a constructive, respectful environment where participants can collaborate regardless of their background, identity, level of experience, or point of view.

Everyone participating in Hoplane's community spaces, including maintainers, is expected to follow this Code of Conduct. Disagreement about technical decisions is welcome; personal attacks are not.

## 2. Where this applies

This policy applies to community spaces operated or moderated for the official Hoplane project, including:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other repository interactions.
- Official project communication channels and community events, if established.
- Public communications made by someone acting in an official capacity on behalf of Hoplane or Kitgenix.

It does not automatically govern independently operated forks, personal accounts, or unrelated communities. Those operators may adopt this policy for their own spaces.

## 3. Expected behaviour

To keep collaboration productive, please:

- Treat other participants with courtesy, patience, and respect.
- Welcome questions and contributions from people with different experience levels.
- Keep reviews focused on code, documentation, evidence, and technical decisions rather than individuals.
- Give useful, specific feedback and accept constructive criticism professionally.
- Respect differing views while remaining open to correction and new information.
- Credit original authors, projects, and sources where appropriate.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and the time of maintainers and contributors.
- Use descriptive issue titles, follow contribution templates, and provide reproduction steps when reporting bugs.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, correct them where possible, and work towards a resolution.

## 4. Unacceptable behaviour

The following conduct is not permitted in official Hoplane community spaces:

- Harassment, bullying, intimidation, stalking, threats, or encouraging violence.
- Discriminatory abuse, slurs, or targeted hostility towards individuals or groups.
- Sexual harassment, unwelcome sexual attention, or inappropriate sexual content.
- Personal insults, sustained trolling, or deliberately disruptive arguments.
- Publishing another person's private information without their explicit permission (including private contact details, credentials, or identifying records).
- Impersonating contributors, maintainers, Kitgenix, or other organisations.
- Spam, unsolicited promotions, misleading links, or repeated off-topic content.
- Knowingly submitting malicious code, deceptive instructions, stolen content, or unauthorised secrets.
- Using project discussions to facilitate phishing, malware, fraud, or other abusive use of short links.
- Retaliating against someone for making a good-faith report or assisting with an investigation.

Not every disagreement or critical review constitutes misconduct. Maintainers will consider context, intent where relevant, impact, and patterns of behaviour.

## 5. Reporting a concern

**Do not publish sensitive conduct allegations or personal information in a public GitHub issue or discussion.**

To report a potential violation, contact the Kitgenix maintainers using an appropriate private contact method listed at **https://kitgenix.com**. Identify the message as **"Hoplane Code of Conduct report"**. If no private contact method is available, you may ask a maintainer in a non-sensitive GitHub message how to submit a report privately; do not include allegation details in that public message.

Helpful information includes:

- A short description of what happened.
- Links to relevant public comments, issues, or pull requests.
- Approximate dates and the affected community space.
- Relevant evidence you are authorised to share.
- Whether there is an ongoing safety or retaliation concern.

Share only the personal information necessary to explain the concern. Reports may be submitted by affected individuals or witnesses.

**Security vulnerabilities are handled separately.** Do not use public community channels to disclose vulnerabilities; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## 6. How reports are handled

Kitgenix maintainers will assess reports in good faith and take action appropriate to the circumstances. Where practical, the process will include:

1. Reviewing the available information and considering any immediate safety concerns.
2. Asking for clarification or additional context when necessary.
3. Allowing affected parties an appropriate opportunity to respond, where safe and reasonable.
4. Determining whether a violation occurred and what response is proportionate.
5. Communicating an outcome to the relevant parties to the extent appropriate.

We aim to handle reports promptly, but cannot promise a fixed investigation or resolution deadline.

Reports and related evidence will be shared only with those who reasonably need access to review or act on them, subject to applicable legal and safety obligations. Absolute confidentiality or anonymity cannot be guaranteed.

Where a report concerns a maintainer, that person should not decide the outcome of their own case. An uninvolved maintainer will handle the matter where one is available; if not, the limitation will be explained to the reporting party where practical.

Knowingly fabricated reports or deliberate misuse of the reporting process may themselves be treated as misconduct. A good-faith report that cannot be substantiated is not a violation.

## 7. Possible moderation actions

Depending on severity and circumstances, maintainers may:

- Clarify expectations or request a change in behaviour.
- Edit, hide, lock, or remove inappropriate comments and contributions where platform permissions allow.
- Issue a private or public warning, as appropriate.
- Temporarily restrict participation or require a cooling-off period.
- Permanently restrict participation in official project spaces.
- Escalate credible threats or unlawful conduct to the platform or relevant authorities where appropriate.

A serious incident may justify immediate restrictions without prior warning. Moderation decisions are limited to channels and resources that Kitgenix controls; Kitgenix cannot impose restrictions on unrelated third-party communities.

Anyone subject to a moderation decision may request a review through the same private contact channel. An uninvolved maintainer will consider the request where available. A review does not guarantee reversal.

## 8. Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers are expected to model these standards, apply them consistently, avoid conflicts of interest, and explain moderation decisions when reasonably possible without compromising privacy or safety.

Maintainers may close issues, decline pull requests, or redirect support enquiries for technical or project-management reasons. These routine decisions do not, by themselves, constitute Code of Conduct enforcement.
